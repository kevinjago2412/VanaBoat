Vana Boat

Overview

Vana Boat is an autonomous and remotely controlled robotic boat designed to help vannamei shrimp farmers monitor pond water quality more efficiently.

Background

Monitoring pond water quality traditionally requires farmers to collect water samples and send them to a laboratory or research facility. This process can take around three days before the results are available.

Vana Boat was developed to make water monitoring more practical by bringing the measurement system directly to the pond. The boat can navigate remotely or operate autonomously while collecting water quality data on-site.

Features

- Remote-controlled operation
- Autonomous operation
- TDS monitoring
- pH monitoring
- Water temperature monitoring
- Automatic feeding system
- On-site water quality monitoring

Technology

- Microcontroller: ESP32
- Communication: LoRa and ESP32 Local Web Server
- Sensors: TDS, pH, and water temperature
- Actuators: Propulsion motors and automatic feeder

Project Status

✅ Completed and tested

The Vana Boat prototype has been assembled and tested to validate its remote control, autonomous operation, water quality monitoring, and automatic feeding functions.
